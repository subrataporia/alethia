Doctor Appointment FAQs

A few answers to help you prepare for booking a consultation.

Call +91 8100381818
How do I book an appointment with a doctor?

Select Book Now on a doctor’s card, then choose the department, doctor, preferred date and a sample time slot on the appointment form. Call the centre to confirm the actual schedule.

Can I filter doctors by department or name?

Yes. Choose a department, enter a doctor’s name, or use both filters together, then select Search. Clear resets the list to all doctors.

Are the doctor profiles and appointment slots confirmed?

The profiles and appointment slots on this demo page are sample information. Please call +91 8100381818 to verify doctor details and availability before visiting.

What should I bring to my consultation?

Bring any referral or prescription, relevant previous reports, and a list of medicines you currently take. Confirm any doctor-specific instructions when booking.
