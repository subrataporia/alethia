Test Preparation

Please follow these basic guidelines before sample collection to ensure quick and accurate results.

No Strict Fasting Required

Routine blood tests like CBC do not require overnight fasting. You may take regular meals unless advised otherwise by your doctor.

Drink Plenty of Water

Staying well hydrated helps keep your veins full, making blood draw smoother and comfortable.

Inform Ongoing Medications

Please inform our phlebotomist if you are currently taking any regular blood thinners or antibiotics.

Comfortable Clothing

Wear loose-fitting clothes with sleeves that roll up easily past the elbow.

Why is This Test Needed?

Diagnostic tests provide critical clinical insights to detect illnesses early and guide appropriate treatment.

Anaemia & Weakness

Identifies low haemoglobin, iron deficiency, or unexplained fatigue and dizziness.

Infection & Fever

Elevated white blood cell count indicates acute viral or bacterial infections.

Preventive Health

Standard routine checkup to assess overall bodily health and immunity status.

Treatment Monitoring

Evaluates how well medical therapy or post-operative recovery is progressing.
