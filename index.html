Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking tests, preparing for your visit, reports, and home collection.

Search Tests

Use the test search above to find the test you need, then follow the booking options shown on the page. You can also contact the centre if you need help choosing a test.

Fasting requirements depend on the specific test. Check the instructions provided when you book, or ask your healthcare professional or the diagnostic centre before your visit.

Home collection can be requested while arranging your booking. Availability may depend on your location, the test, and the appointment slot.

Report delivery options and expected timelines vary by test. Please check the details provided at booking or contact the centre for an update on your report.

Some tests may require a prescription or referral. Bring one if your doctor has provided it, and confirm any test-specific requirements when booking.
